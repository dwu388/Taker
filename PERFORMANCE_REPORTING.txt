V24 PERFORMANCE REPORTING
=========================

Runtime report
--------------
The collector automatically maintains:

    data/CURRENT_MODEL_PERFORMANCE.txt

Default cadence: every 60 minutes after a successful collection cycle.

The cadence is configured in axiom_migrated_config.json:

    performance_report.enabled
    performance_report.interval_minutes
    performance_report.output_path
    performance_report.benchmark_db
    performance_report.recent_events

The report can also be regenerated immediately with:

    performance_report.bat

or:

    python -m profit_taker.performance_report --force

What the report calls a success
-------------------------------
- A forecast promotion is a validated success only when a challenger passes its
  one-use out-of-sample promotion cohort and the V24 promotion rules.
- A policy promotion is a validated policy success only when the separate policy
  challenger passes its own one-use promotion test.
- Positive execution-conservative paper returns are reported as paper successes,
  but are kept separate from prospective audit evidence.
- Positive execution-equity performance in the isolated $1,000 benchmark is shown
  as end-to-end strategy evidence, together with drawdown.
- A clean collection integrity gate is shown as a data-quality success, not as
  evidence of trading alpha.

What the report calls a failure or unresolved result
----------------------------------------------------
- Rejected forecast or policy challengers are shown explicitly as failures of the
  challenger/promotion test rather than being hidden.
- Losing/non-positive paper trades and negative execution-average returns are shown
  explicitly.
- Benchmark losses/drawdown and cancelled pending entries are surfaced.
- Missing sealed-audit evidence, no closed paper trades, or no promoted policy are
  reported as unresolved evidence. They are not counted as successes merely because
  the system has collected many rows or has run for many days.
- Collector failures, row-count mismatches, payload-integrity errors and identity
  conflicts are shown separately as data-quality failures.

Main sections
-------------
1. Executive performance summary
2. Collection / data quality
3. Forecast model registry, promotions and prediction provenance
4. Sealed prospective audit metrics
5. Calibration and sequence challenger status
6. Policy registry, policy promotions and counterfactual-target maturity
7. Paper-trading returns, win rate, reward, MFE/MAE, peak capture and close reasons
8. Isolated $1,000 benchmark execution equity, return, drawdown and position results
9. Interpretation guide explaining exactly what each success/failure signal means

Important
---------
The runtime SQLite databases are intentionally excluded from Git. Therefore the
repository itself does not contain live performance numbers. The report is populated
from the current local V24 databases when the collector/report command runs.
