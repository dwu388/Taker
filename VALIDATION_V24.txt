V24 VALIDATION / RELEASE GATE

  data\axiom_v24_raw.sqlite

  .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest

Fresh-collection smoke test:
  run_axiom_once.bat
  collection_status.bat

collection_status reports:
  ready_to_collect=true
  session_marked=true
  sqlite_integrity_ok=true
  raw_payload_complete=true
  raw_payload_integrity_errors=0
  identity_ok=true
  cycle_row_count_mismatches=0
  cycles_outside_active_session=0
  attempts_outside_active_session=0
  successful_attempt_cycle_count_difference=0

Model/status checks use the same canonical raw DB:
  .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m profit_taker.axiom_v24 status --db data\axiom_v24_raw.sqlite

Only after sufficient real history exists, exercise as appropriate:
  .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m profit_taker.axiom_v24 bootstrap --db data\axiom_v24_raw.sqlite
  .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m profit_taker.axiom_v24 maintain --db data\axiom_v24_raw.sqlite

  .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m profit_taker.axiom_v24 train-policy --db data\axiom_v24_raw.sqlite

Review invariants:
  - clipboard capture is primed with a unique verified sentinel before every Ctrl+C;
  - a successful capture is atomic and stores exactly one durable observation per parsed card;
  - exact accepted clipboard bytes are retained with SHA-256 and zlib compression;
  - failed attempts cannot masquerade as successful capture heartbeats/token absence;
  - canonical raw observation source is axiom_observations;
  - full-mint identity collisions are hard failures;
  - database/capture/provenance IDs and repair-version metadata are excluded from model features;
  - peak_at and confirmed_at both remain in horizon-safe targets;
  - token role is permanently assigned at first seen;
  - paper entries/exits fill only on a later observable snapshot;
  - long-history sequence cache invalidates from a late historical insertion forward;
  - sealed audit scoring uses audit-born token lifetime membership, confirmation-safe truth, and token-balanced metrics;
  - policy cohort/token refresh helpers receive the active V24Config.

Interpretation:
  Passing this gate means the repository is suitable to collect a fresh authoritative
  research dataset and later conduct leakage-hardened paper evaluation. It does not
  by itself demonstrate profitable alpha or authorize real-money autonomous trading.
