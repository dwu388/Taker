Review invariants:
  - clipboard capture is primed with a unique verified sentinel before every Ctrl+C;
  - a successful capture is atomic and stores exactly one durable observation per parsed card;
  - exact accepted clipboard bytes are retained with SHA-256 and zlib compression;
  - failed attempts cannot masquerade as successful capture heartbeats/token absence;
  - canonical raw observation source is axiom_observations;
  - full-mint identity collisions are hard failures;
  - database/capture/provenance IDs and repair-version metadata are excluded from model features;
  - active forecast, survival, sequence and promotion-required horizons never exceed 1440 minutes;
  - the Axiom 25th hour is buffer-only and cannot become target truth;
  - age >= 1440 minutes is natural model age-out, not operational death;
  - peak_at and confirmed_at both remain horizon-safe targets;
  - changing from the former 72h target forces a rebuild of derived finalized labels from raw observations;
  - old 72h forecaster/policy target hashes are not warm-compatible with the 24h generation;
  - token role is permanently assigned at first seen;
  - promotion maturity remains token-lifetime + outcome-horizon safe (approximately 24h + 24h after cohort end);
  - paper entries/exits fill only on a later observable snapshot;
  - long-history sequence cache invalidates from a late historical insertion forward;
  - sealed audit scoring uses audit-born token lifetime membership, confirmation-safe truth, and token-balanced metrics;
  - policy cohort/token refresh helpers receive the active V24Config;
  - collector/readiness, performance-reporting, liquidity/friction and benchmark isolation remain unchanged by the target conversion.

Interpretation:
  Passing this gate means the repository is suitable to collect a fresh authoritative
  25-hour-view dataset for a 24-hour V24 lifecycle and later conduct leakage-hardened
  paper evaluation. It does not by itself demonstrate profitable alpha or authorize
  real-money autonomous trading.
